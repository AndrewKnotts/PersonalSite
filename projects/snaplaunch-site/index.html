snaplaunch Website
A visually engaging marketing site crafted to introduce Snaplaunch — an AI agent platform — through clear messaging, interactive features, and a sleek, tech aesthetic.
Go to website
YEAR
2025
COMPANY

SCOPE
BRAND IDENTITY, UI/UX DESIGN, FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT
Overview
I designed and built the Snaplaunch marketing site from scratch, handling everything from layout and animation to deployment. The goal was to create an inviting, intuitive introduction to the product using vibrant visuals, clean typography, and a bold color palette. Microinteractions, scroll effects, and subtle animations bring the experience to life.
Outcome
The site is now live as Snaplaunch’s primary landing page. It actively supports the sales team by clearly communicating the product’s value and key use cases. As the platform evolves, I continually update the site with fresh content to keep it aligned and engaging.
