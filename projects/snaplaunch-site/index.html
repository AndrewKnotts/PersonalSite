snaplaunch Website
A playful, branded one-page marketing site designed and developed to introduce Snaplaunch — an AI agent platform — through clear visuals, dynamic interaction, and an approachable, tech-forward identity.
Go to website
YEAR
2025
COMPANY
ERANOVA
SCOPE
BRAND IDENTITY, UI/UX DESIGN, FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT
Overview
I designed and developed the Snaplaunch marketing website from the ground up, handling everything from brand direction and UI design to full frontend implementation. The goal was to introduce Snaplaunch in a way that felt intuitive, inviting, and visually alive, with a tone that balanced credibility and creativity. The site uses a consistent visual system of spirals, gradients, and clean typography, anchored in a vibrant purplish-pink palette. Microinteractions, scroll-based animations, and chat modals help bring the product to life on the page, while reinforcing its flexibility and ease of use.
Problem
We needed a site that clearly communicates what Snaplaunch is, how it works, and why it matters, all while establishing trust and giving users a preview of the actual product experience — without making it feel dry or overly technical.
Design & Engineering Process
Created initial wireframes and layout flows in Figma, emphasizing a friendly and clear visual hierarchy. Built the structure around three simple steps: Brand, Train, and Launch — each illustrated with custom animations. Designed looping examples of real chat agents in action using mockups and an animated carousel. Integrated animated transitions and scroll-triggered effects to guide users through the page in a fluid, engaging way. Focused on giving the site personality while keeping the design minimal and mobile-friendly.Developed the full frontend solo using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Built interactive UI components, including animated modals, scrolling effects, and custom carousels. Ensured full responsive behavior and optimized performance across devices. Structured the codebase for easy updates as the product and brand continue to evolve.
Outcome
The site is now live and serves as Snaplaunch’s main landing page. It’s already helped convert early users by making the product feel clear, modern, and easy to understand. The combination of expressive branding and interactive storytelling helps position Snaplaunch as an accessible, well-crafted AI platform — and the site continues to grow as we refine the product’s messaging.
