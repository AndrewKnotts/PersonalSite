Eranova Application
A long-term redesign and refactor of the core Eranova platform — transforming a functional but fragmented internal AI tool into a clean, usable product with scalable UI patterns, responsive layouts, and a unified visual system.
YEAR
2025
COMPANY
ERANOVA
SCOPE
PRODUCT DESIGN, UI/UX DESIGN, FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT
Overview
The Eranova Platform is where enterprise users build, train, and manage AI agents for use cases like lease abstraction, document parsing, and customer support. When I joined the team, the platform already existed — but the interface was visually inconsistent, hard to navigate, and challenging to scale. My role has been to lead the design and frontend overhaul: Rethinking layouts and component hierarchies Designing a system around modularity and clarity Rebuilding the frontend architecture with reusable patterns While the platform is still evolving, my work has been central in defining its product experience and visual foundation — setting a standard for how the team builds going forward.
Problem
The original platform had grown organically — features were added fast, but design hadn’t kept up. Key problems: Inconsistent UI elements with no reusable structure Poor readability of data-dense views Disjointed layouts that didn’t support scalable workflows Limited visual feedback for key interactions (e.g. saving, training, uploading) The goal was to bring everything under a unified visual and component system, improve usability across the board, and make the platform feel more intuitive, responsive, and professional.
Design & Engineering Process
My first contribution was a new analytics dashboard. This project gave me hands-on experience with the platform’s internal logic and helped me define foundational patterns — such as consistent grid structure, card layouts, hover states, and visual hierarchy. From there, I led the gradual redesign of core modules: Agent creation and training flows File management and document parsing Calendar and chat history views Organization and permissions settings I worked iteratively — often designing and implementing features simultaneously — and built high-fidelity mockups and design specs in Figma, always with a focus on clarity, ease of use, and technical feasibility. Designing with incomplete product direction: I often had to work without full specs — which meant prototyping, proposing solutions, and leading design conversations. Balancing legacy logic with new UI: Since we couldn’t rebuild everything from scratch, I had to wrap new interfaces around older backend structures without breaking functionality. Scaling the system: I designed components to be modular and themeable, so the team could easily apply them to future features.
Outcome
The platform is still in active development — but since the redesign began: The app has become significantly more navigable and cohesive Internal users report faster onboarding and better usability The frontend is cleaner, faster, and easier to maintain The work also laid the groundwork for Snaplaunch and other future Eranova products. My contributions here reflect a deep understanding of product thinking — not just how things look, but how they work, how they scale, and how users experience them over time.
