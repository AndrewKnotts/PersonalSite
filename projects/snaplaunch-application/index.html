CASE STUDY
HUGEM Innovation Challenge SEA IV
Bold and future-forward visual identity for a 3-day youth conference hosted in Singapore.
YEAR
2024
Client
The Global Citizen Education Group
Scope of work
Visual Identity, Event Design
Overview
In partnership with The Global Citizen Academy and the Harvard Undergraduate Global Education Movement, the HUGEM Innovation Challenge Southeast Asia IV brings together delegates from across South East Asia and esteemed speakers for a 3-day conference in Singapore. This year, guided by the Sustainable Development Goals 11 — Sustainable Cities & Communities and 12 — Responsible Consumption & Production, we aspire to help our brightest-mind delegates think deeply about these global challenges and offer solutions on how young people may collaborate to foster a sustainable international development.
