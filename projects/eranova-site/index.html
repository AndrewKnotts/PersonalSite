Eranova Website
A full rebrand and interactive web experience for Eranova, creating a bold visual identity that introduces the platform with clarity and personality.

YEAR
2025
COMPANY
ERANOVA
SCOPE
BRAND IDENTITY, UI/UX DESIGN, FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT
Overview
I redesigned Eranova’s brand and website from the ground up, handling the entire design and development myself. The goal was to communicate the company’s cutting-edge AI technology with clarity, credibility, and a modern edge. The result is a sleek, fully responsive site with interactive visuals and a consistent design language across every page.
Outcome
The site redefined Eranova’s digital presence, acting as a bold brand statement that positions the company as innovative and credible in a competitive AI landscape. The site's modular design makes it easy to scale internally, while externally, it supports hiring and strengthens client-facing interactions.
